# Voice provenance

Model: `vits-piper-id_ID-news_tts-medium`, from the public
[sherpa-onnx TTS models release](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models).
Model configuration identifies `id_ID`, Indonesian, `news_tts`, one speaker,
22,050 Hz. Inference runs locally; the finished narration is resampled to 48 kHz.
It is a synthetic generic narrator, not a clone of a named person.

The archive's included MODEL_CARD is inconsistent with its configuration: it
describes a Malayalam model. It is retained as a provenance issue; no claim of
verified commercial licensing is made. Do not redistribute the model archive
as part of this project. The generated voice is a review production track.

The script and rendered voice stem are editable/replaceable independently of
the original music and SFX. Music is newly synthesized for this project, without
sampling the user's motion reference or any commercial song.
